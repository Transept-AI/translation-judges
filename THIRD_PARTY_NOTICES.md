# Third-party material

## LAIT (Ferstler et al., 2026)

`prompts/reader_mirror.md` uses, close to verbatim, the description of poor and good wording from the LAIT
annotation guidelines, and the four reader ratings (acceptability, smoothness, immersion, continue reading) with
their 1–5 anchors from the LAIT questionnaire. `calibration.json` holds the mean and spread of our judges over the
LAIT evaluation passages. We cite the study for both:

> Ferstler, Y. et al. (2026). *AI translation of literary texts is "fine", but readers still prefer human
> translations.* arXiv:2606.26040. Code and supplement: https://github.com/Yves575/lait
